Let's put rings in your product.

Questions

What do I get with early access?

Your dashboard login, tokens for your apps and websites, and direct help from us setting up your first story groups.

Do I need a developer?

Only once, to add a few lines of code. After that your team runs everything from the dashboard.

Which platforms are supported?

Websites and React today. iOS, Android and React Native SDKs are coming soon. See platform support.

Can I use my own brand colours?

Yes. Rings, badges and titles can all be styled to match your brand. Try the ring designer.
