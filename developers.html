For developers

A few lines of code. That's it.

Load the SDK, pass your token and choose where the story bar goes. Content, order and styling all come from the dashboard, so you integrate once and never touch it again.

Quick start

Live in three steps

  1. Add your app or website in the dashboard and copy its token.
  2. Load the SDK and place an empty container where the story bar should go.
  3. Call Storyrings.init with your token. Done.

Options and callbacks

      
Hook stories into your own app

      
Callbacks let your code react when someone opens a story or taps a button, for deep links, analytics or anything else.

    

    

      

        
OptionWhat it does

        
          
tokenYour app or website token from the dashboard. Required.

          
containerWhere the story bar is placed on the page.

          
onLoadRuns when story groups have loaded.

          
onFailRuns if stories couldn't be loaded, so you can hide the bar.

          
onStoryOpenRuns when someone opens a story group.

          
onCtaClickRuns when someone taps a story's button. Use it for deep links.
