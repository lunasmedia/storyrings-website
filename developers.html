For developers

A few lines of code. That's it.

Load the SDK, pass your token and choose where the story bar goes. Content, order and styling all come from the dashboard, so you integrate once and never touch it again.

Quick start

Live in three steps

  1. Add your app or website in the dashboard and copy its token.
  2. Load the SDK and place an empty container where the story bar should go.
  3. Call Stories.init with your token, then mount the bar on your container. Done.

Options and callbacks

      
Hook stories into your own app

      
init creates one client per token. mount places a story bar and takes the styling and callbacks. Callbacks let your code react when someone opens a story or taps a button, for deep links, analytics or anything else.

    

    
Stories.init(options)

    

      

        
OptionDefaultWhat it does

        
          
tokenrequiredYour app or website token from the dashboard.

          
apiUrlscript originYour Stories server. Only needed if you host it yourself.

          
analyticstrueSend impression, view and click events.

          
storagelocalStorageWhere "viewed" state, the cached feed and the anonymous viewer ID are kept. null disables all three.

          
reachtrueCount unique viewers with an anonymous ID. Pass false until consent if your privacy rules require it.

        
      

    

    
client.mount(target, options)

    

      

        
OptionDefaultWhat it does

        
          
barthe main barWhich story bar to show, by its key.

          
tagsnoneThe viewer's tags, so groups with an audience only show to the right people.

          
themesee dashboardRing size, colours, titles and the rest of the bar's styling.

          
ctaTarget'_self'How story buttons open if you don't handle them yourself.

          
transition'slide'How the full-screen viewer opens: 'slide' or 'zoom'.

          
openSharedLinkstrueOpen the story a shared link points to when the page loads.

        
      

    

    
Callbacks, passed to mount

    

      

        
CallbackWhat it does

        
          
onLoadRuns once the feed loads, with the published groups.

          
onFailRuns if stories couldn't be loaded. The bar hides itself.

          
onStoryOpenRuns every time a story starts.

          
onCloseRuns when the viewer closes, telling you whether the person closed it or watched to the end.

          
onCtaClickRuns when someone taps a story's button. Return false to handle the link yourself.

          
onShareRuns when someone shares a story. Return false to use your own share menu.
