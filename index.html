01

Add your app or website

Register it by bundle ID or URL. Each one gets its own token, its own stories and its own analytics.

02

Create story groups

Each group is one ring. Upload images, videos and GIFs, add buttons and links, then flip the switch to go live.

03

Drop in the SDK

Paste your token, place the story bar, and your rings appear. Change content from the dashboard any time, with no app release.

Everything included

Stories that feel native, without the build

Full-screen viewer

Tap to skip, hold to pause, swipe between groups, progress bars and muted autoplay video, all built in.

Rings you can style

Gradients, glows, rotating rings, badges like NEW or SALE, and animated GIF covers that catch the eye.

Simple dashboard

Create groups, drag to reorder, schedule start and end dates, and turn a whole group on or off with one switch.

Buttons that convert

Add a call-to-action to any story and send viewers to a product, a page or a deep link in your app.

Built-in analytics

See impressions, opens, completions and button clicks for every group and every story.

Fast and accessible

A lightweight script, keyboard and screen-reader support, and reduced motion respected automatically.
