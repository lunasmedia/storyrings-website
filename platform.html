Story viewer

Full-screen stories that feel native

The viewer behaves the way people expect from their favourite social apps, so there's nothing to learn.

  • Tap to skip, hold to pause, swipe between groups
  • Images, video and GIFs in 9:16, with muted autoplay
  • A call-to-action button on any story
  • Keyboard and screen-reader friendly

Dashboard

Run your stories without a developer

Your marketing team creates, schedules and publishes. Changes show up in your app or website straight away, with no release needed.

  • Story groups as cards, with drag-to-reorder
  • One switch to put a whole group live or take it down
  • Schedule start and end dates for campaigns
  • Separate apps and websites, each with its own token

  • Button clicks for every call-to-action
  • Results per app, per group and per story
  • Events you can send to your own analytics tools
